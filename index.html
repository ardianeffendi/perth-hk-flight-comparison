The verdict

At $736 all-in vs $776.99 (AirAsia + baggage only), Singapore Airlines is now cheaper once you add even just a checked bag — and you get full-service, meals, and Changi Airport included.

AirAsia only wins if you travel carry-on only ($576.99). The moment you add a 20 kg bag ($200 return), SQ is already $41 cheaper — and far better on every front.
