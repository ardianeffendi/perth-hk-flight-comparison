The verdict

At $736 all-in vs $776.99 (AirAsia + baggage only), Singapore Airlines is now cheaper once you add even just a checked bag — and you get meals, Changi Airport, and proper full-service included.
AirAsia only wins if you travel carry-on only ($576.99). The moment you add a 20 kg bag ($200 return), SQ is already $40 cheaper — and far better on every other metric.
With the AirAsia full bundle (meal + 20 kg bag + duty-free voucher + 1 change fee) at $300 extra, total hits $876.99 — that's $141 more than SQ for a worse experience.
Traveloka fares can differ from booking direct with SQ. Worth double-checking at singaporeair.com to confirm $736 is still available for your exact dates before committing.
